# Nature targeted revision: manuscript notes

These are **results-supported drafting notes**, not a decision to submit or to replace the locked main-figure portfolio. All analyses are post hoc and concern hypothetical stated incremental consumption. Use `NATURE_REVISION_RESULTS.md` and the locked `NATURE_EMPIRICAL_RESULTS.md` for exact evidence and uncertainty.

## Introduction: conceptual structure to retain

Latent variation in behavioural responses matters for economic description and policy design. Richer measurements could, in principle, reveal stable response types, yet the present survey finds that detailed baseline information predicts little of an individual's stated marginal-consumption response. The broader alternative is not merely that mean responses depend on context: the limited observable signal may have different predictive meaning under different randomized resource-delivery forms within one decision domain. Analogies to money, health, education, energy, or digital choice can motivate why this distinction matters across domains, but this study tests only the three transfer forms in its survey. Problem recognition or representation may be a theoretical motivation, **not** an identified mechanism; the study does not measure attention, representations, or causal psychological mediators. Do not present Bordalo et al. (2026) as an empirically verified mechanism here.

## Abstract / conclusion language within the evidence

> Rich measurement did little to solve the prediction problem for stated consumption responses, and the limited observable signal was only weakly portable across randomized transfer contexts. The challenge is therefore not only how deeply people are measured, but whether what is learned about them retains predictive meaning when the context changes.

A short closing sentence is: **More information about people does not guarantee more portable predictions of behaviour.** This remains a claim about observed stated responses in this experiment, not stable preferences or realized spending.

The targeted domain analysis does **not** warrant: "Medical responses are driven by security" or any analogous domain-specific causal mechanism. The clearest domain-drop contribution is household needs in all three forms, with no validated between-form domain difference; the ridge map does not corroborate one broad driver. A defensible Results sentence is:

> Although form-specific predictive mappings differ in aggregate, broad-domain deletion and standardized ridge maps did not consistently localize those differences to one observable predictor domain.

## Measurement paragraph

> The hypothetical design permits clean randomization of institutional context while holding the underlying response question fixed and collecting unusually rich baseline information. The results therefore speak directly to the predictability of stated behavioural responses under controlled scenarios. Whether realized spending exhibits the same predictive structure remains an open empirical question.

One permissible conditional sensitivity sentence is:

> If the classical independent-error model held and outcome reliability were as low as 0.30, an observed ALL-model R² of 0.048 would imply a latent-response R² around 0.161; most latent variation would still be unexplained by this predictor set.

Immediately follow this with: **"Outcome reliability was not measured; this calculation is a scenario, not a correction or ceiling."** The observed subjective increment below +0.01 R² does not necessarily remain below +0.01 after dividing by reliability 0.30. Q1/Q2 screens are empirical sample restrictions, not reliability measurements. Do not assert that hypothetical responses are harder or easier to predict than realized behaviour.

## Policy and HTE paragraph

> Out-of-fold personalization did not improve the pooled three-form, all-amount stated-consumption objective relative to uniform Cash. Its Food-selected and Medical-selected groups had negative point estimates for held-out average DR contrasts relative to Cash, and their additive contributions reproduced the negative overall policy gain.

Qualify the sentence immediately:

> At RMB 1,000, a separate exploratory Cash-versus-Medical rule had small positive gain point estimates across three folds, although its DR intervals included zero. The pooled null therefore should not be extrapolated to all amount-specific targeting problems.

Do not call the policy analysis welfare, actual stimulus, or advice to deploy Cash. Predictable Medical−Cash HTE remains quality-sensitive and amount-specific; the current revision does not undo that gate.

## Figure placement and claim gate

- Revision Fig. R3 (reliability) and Fig. R4 (policy decomposition) are useful candidates for the main text or a tightly linked supplement, because they answer major interpretation threats. R4 must retain the positive-point RMB 1,000 qualifier.
- Revision Fig. R1 and ED Fig. R2 are descriptive Extended Data/Supplement candidates. The manuscript should **not** claim that a particular observable domain explains context-dependent prediction, because the direct context comparisons and second representation do not pass the work file's gate.
- The original six Nature figures are unchanged pending review. No new broad moderator or feature search is justified from these outputs.

## Claims to avoid

Avoid "the true determinants of behaviour are a function of context," "big-data personalization is an illusion," "preferences are unstable," "context causes recategorization," and any assertion that this hypothetical design is stronger evidence than realized behaviour. Do not treat selected predicted-positive shares as prevalence of true individual benefit, ridge coefficients as mechanisms, or sample restrictions as reliability estimates. No national-representativeness claim can be made from the present margins and online adult sample frame.
