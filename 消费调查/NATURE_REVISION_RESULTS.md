# Nature targeted revision: results

The locked Nature results and core estimands are unchanged. This targeted, post hoc revision adds three reviewer-facing diagnostics for the hypothetical **stated** marginal-consumption response. Reported intervals are 95% with their method specified in `NATURE_REVISION_AUDIT.md`; they are not all interchangeable coverage claims. Exact numbers are in `tables/nature_revision_*.csv`, and four candidate figures and source tables are in `figures/` and `tables/`.

## 1. Where does the limited predictive signal move?

The seven-domain held-out RF map does **not** cleanly localize the cross-form portability difference. The largest positive domain is household needs/exposure in **all three** randomized contexts: dropping it lowers OOF R² by 0.0114 in Cash (respondent-bootstrap interval 0.0029–0.0207), 0.0139 in Food (0.0040–0.0241), and 0.0149 in Medical (0.0033–0.0264). These are small absolute gains, compatible with the locked ALL-model R² near 0.05. The direct Cash−Food, Cash−Medical, and Food−Medical needs-importance differences are −0.0025 (−0.0156 to 0.0103), −0.0035 (−0.0176 to 0.0118), and −0.0010 (−0.0163 to 0.0133): none localizes a context shift. The intervals include validation uncertainty conditional on repeated fits, while seed-level ranges are supplied separately.

Resources/position contributes 0.0031 in Cash, 0.0017 in Food, and approximately zero in Medical; liquidity, expectations, security/pressure, and broader attitudes are all near zero or negative under domain drop, with intervals spanning zero. Demographics/exposure is 0.0071 in Cash (−0.0001 to 0.0142), 0.0011 in Food, and 0.0055 in Medical, but its direct context differences also span zero. Negative domain-drop values are reported rather than truncated. None of the 21 direct domain-by-context difference intervals excludes zero. Figure R1 shows the full map with a common scale.

The secondary standardized ridge coefficients differ visibly across forms for selected individual encoded predictors, but the fixed pooled top-20 heatmap is descriptive. Every **domain-level mean absolute coefficient** context-difference percentile interval includes zero. This second predictive representation therefore does not corroborate a stable, substantively interpretable domain-specific movement. The correct interpretation gate is to retain the locked **aggregate observed-portability** result and say the current data do not cleanly identify which broad observable domain drives it. Feature coefficients and domain-drop importance are neither attention weights nor causal mechanisms. The fixed top-20 ridge display is revision ED Fig. R2; all coefficients and bootstrap differences are in the ridge table.

## 2. Could low R² be only outcome noise?

The survey has no repeated stated-MPC outcome, so outcome reliability is **not estimated**. Conditional on classical independent additive outcome error, the locked ALL RF R² 0.0482 would correspond to latent-response R² 0.1607 at assumed reliability 0.30, 0.1205 at 0.40, 0.0803 at 0.60, and 0.0603 at 0.80. At r=0.30, the transformed locked fold-bootstrap endpoint interval is 0.1348–0.1851. Thus even this severe assumed unreliability leaves at least approximately 81% of latent response variation unexplained under this model and interval calculation. This is **conditional sensitivity**, not a factual correction, a bound, or a statement about all possible predictors.

The objective/needs O model rises from observed 0.0465 to 0.1551, 0.1163, 0.0775, and 0.0582 at those same reliability values. The small O+S-minus-O increment rises from observed 0.00343 to 0.01145 at r=0.30 (transformed interval 0.00033–0.02238), 0.00858 at r=0.40 (0.00024–0.01678), 0.00572 at r=0.60, and 0.00429 at r=0.80. Accordingly, the locked empirical statement that subjective information adds less than +0.01 **observed** OOS R² must not be silently translated into a <+0.01 latent-response increment under severe unreliability.

The separate empirical quality-screen comparison is ALL OOF R² 0.0496 in R (N=5,497), 0.0449 in C (N=5,171), 0.0226 in Q1 (N=2,715), and 0.0100 in Q2 (N=1,208). These restrictions do not estimate reliability and may also change sample composition and outcome variance. Figure R3 keeps the assumed sensitivity and empirical screens separate.

## 3. Why does full-sample personalization fail?

The OOF three-form argmax rule assigns 60.4% to Cash, 29.6% to Food, and 10.1% to Medical. Its exact cross-fitted DR gain over uniform Cash is −0.00873 midpoint stated MPC (paired interval −0.01915 to +0.00195), reproducing the locked −0.00873 result. Food-selected respondents have a held-out DR Food−Cash average contrast of −0.0189 (−0.0519 to +0.0131), contributing −0.00559 (−0.01539 to +0.00400) to the population policy gain. Medical-selected respondents have a Medical−Cash contrast of −0.0312 (−0.0860 to +0.0205), contributing −0.00314 (−0.00869 to +0.00203). The two contributions add exactly to −0.00873. The policy does not merely choose non-Cash forms rarely: under this validation, the groups it selects for those forms do not show positive **average** realized randomized contrasts. Individual true rankings remain unidentified.

Predicted crossing is not validated benefit. OOF Food−Cash predictions are positive for 33.6% of respondents, yet their DR subgroup average Food−Cash contrast is −0.0247 (−0.0539 to +0.0044). OOF Medical−Cash predictions are positive for 16.2%, yet their DR subgroup Medical−Cash contrast is −0.0250 (−0.0689 to +0.0190). These intervals do not rule out modest gains, but the negative points and full policy decomposition are consistent with ranking/misallocation noise plus limited average headroom. The data cannot decompose those causes into true individual benefit prevalence versus identification error.

The explicitly exploratory **RMB 1,000 Cash-versus-Medical** stress test provides an important qualification. In the two-arm N=1,207 sample, the OOF rule selects Medical for 25.8–26.4% across three prespecified seeds. DR gains versus uniform Cash are +0.0110 (−0.0026 to +0.0249), +0.0086 (−0.0048 to +0.0233), and +0.0125 (−0.0008 to +0.0267). IPW gains are +0.0072 (−0.0097 to +0.0247), +0.0116 (−0.0052 to +0.0290), and +0.0195 (+0.0026 to +0.0377). Thus the best-case point estimates are consistently positive, but DR intervals span zero and IPW validation is not uniform across seeds. The full-sample claim should be **narrowed** to: the learned three-form rule does not outperform all Cash for the pooled stated-MPC objective. It should not be generalized to every amount-specific two-form targeting problem. Figure R4 presents the decomposition and this stress test on distinct panels.

## 4. Sample composition robustness

Across the ten halves of the five broad age, education, income, hukou, and city-tier splits, Medical−Cash ordinal point contrasts remain negative (from −0.146 to −0.423). Food−Cash point contrasts are also negative but often imprecise. ALL-model subgroup OOF R² ranges from −0.0037 in the lower-education half (N=1,159) to 0.0583 in non-agricultural-hukou respondents (N=2,818). These non-independent, overlapping strata do not justify population transport, and there is no pseudo-national reweighting. Exact N, cell minima, effects, intervals, and R² appear in `nature_revision_composition.csv`.

## Final revision disposition

1. **What genuinely strengthens the Nature story:** the low *observed* predictability is not erased by a wide range of clearly assumed classical outcome-reliability values; the policy-value null now has an exact OOF DR decomposition showing negative point contributions from both selected non-Cash forms. These make the measurement and actionability claims more transparent, not more causal.
2. **What remains Extended Data only:** the domain map and ridge heatmap do not pass the two-representation gate for locating a context-specific driver; the RMB 1,000 Medical targeting stress test is exploratory, positive pointwise, and too imprecise in DR to become a headline policy-success claim. Broad composition strata are supplementary.
3. **What reviewer request should be rejected on methodological grounds:** estimating outcome reliability from subjective-predictor Cronbach alpha, claiming SHAP/domain importance identifies mechanism, inferring individual true beneficiaries from predicted crossings, or constructing "nationally representative" weights without a defensible joint target frame.
4. **Should the core claim change?** Keep the core claim about low observed prediction and weak portability **unchanged**. Strengthen its measurement caveat and **narrow** the pooled personalization null to the actual three-form, all-amount stated-consumption estimand; do not claim universal policy failure or context-caused recategorization.
