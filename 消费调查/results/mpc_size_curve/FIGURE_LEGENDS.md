# Figure legends and source keys

All panels describe hypothetical **stated** consumption responses, not realized MPC. Different respondents occupy different cells. Lines connect randomized discrete doses only. CIs are pointwise, unadjusted for multiplicity; formal five-test Holm values are in slope tables.

1. **Core size curves** (`fig1_ordinal.png`, `fig1_midpoint.png`). Raw nine-cell means ±1.96 sample-mean SE. Ordinal category 1–6 is not cardinal MPC; midpoint weights are 0/.05/.175/.375/.625/.875. Horizontal log spacing reflects fivefold amount steps. Source: `fig1_source.csv`, one row per form×amount; `ordinal_mean/se`, `midpoint_mean/se`, N.
2. **Distribution anatomy** (`fig2_distribution.png`). Six-category unconditional shares stacked within each form×amount. Category 1=basically no extra spending, 2=<10%, 3=10–25%, 4=25–50%, 5=50–75%, 6=>75%; use original questionnaire bins rather than exact individual percentages. Source: `fig2_source.csv`, p1–p6. No respondent-level transition interpretation.
3. **Threshold curves** (`fig3_thresholds.png`). Probability ±1.96 Bernoulli SE for ordinal categories >=2/3/4/5/6. Panels mean any additional spending, >=10%, >=25%, >=50%, top category. Panel vertical scales differ and are labelled probabilities. Source: `fig3_source.csv`, threshold/form/amount/probability/se/N. Formal slope contrasts are in `threshold_slope_contrasts.csv`.
4. **Slope robustness forest** (`fig4_slopes.png`). Three coding panels, all six estimands in R/A/C/Q1/Q2, unadjusted HC3 normal-Wald 95% CIs. Unit is change per fivefold increase. Medical−Food is secondary. No cross-panel comparison of raw unit magnitudes. Source: `fig4_source.csv`, outcome/sample/estimand/slope/lo/hi/N.
5. **Mechanism checks** (`fig5_mechanisms.png`). Left: equal-amount pooled saturated ordinal Food−Cash level gap. Center: ordinal Food−Cash amount slope. Four screens: all, assigned strict, common strict across all amounts, common strict clean. Assigned screen changes eligible population with amount; common screen fixes it. Right: raw ordinal Medical−Cash gaps across amounts, grouped by prior annual self-paid medical expenditure; CIs use independent group-mean SEs. Smaller high-need group has wider CIs; not causal need effects. Sources: `fig5_food_source.csv` (screen/outcome/estimand/effect/se), `fig5_medical_source.csv` (need_group/amount/gap/se and both arm means/SEs/N). No hypothesis was chosen from the plotted subgroups.
