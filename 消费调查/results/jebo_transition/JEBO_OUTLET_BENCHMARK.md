## Architecture chosen

Retain nine short substantive sections, an economic-question opening, an approximately1,500–2,000-word introduction, three main figures and three tables. Lead with cells and within-Cash slopes; make cross-form uncertainty visible in the abstract and inference section. Put model-history and broad robustness in the supplement. A conceptual prediction table is sufficient because no structural parameters are identified. A conditional-response accounting result belongs beside distribution plots, not in a mechanism section.

The visible journal examples support question-led behavioral exposition and explicit measurement boundaries. They do not demonstrate that weak adjusted interaction evidence is enough for publication. This paper therefore emphasizes a bounded distributional extension rather than a discovered law. Published-page ranges span9–44 pages; typography and appendices vary too much to infer a useful universal word target. No fabricated median abstract/table count is reported.
