The Cash extensive margin is descriptively stable, whereas conditional means fall. Fuster et al. provide a different extensive pattern, but this is not a clean contradiction: their filter, horizon and permitted responses differ. The closest German factorial predecessor shows broadly similar margin accounting, so the decomposition is explanatory rather than a new discovery. Realized lottery and Korean programme results support size dependence under different identification and measurement conditions. Recent Italian survey evidence makes horizon and household resources central.

Our restricted-use scenarios add a specific institutional dimension; merely combining form and size is not new. Neither a cross-country mechanism test nor a quantitative parameter comparison is warranted. Sources, designs and boundaries are in mpc_size_literature_benchmark.csv. No numerical cross-study test, exchange-rate conversion or new survey contrast is performed.
