# JEBO literature map

Access date: 2026-10-03. The matrix contains 53 papers: 41 core/measurement papers plus 12 recent JEBO outlet comparators. Adjacent outlet examples are marked D and are not used to inflate the manuscript reference list. Bibliographic records were checked against Crossref and primary publisher/author pages; access scope is recorded row by row. No claim of 53 full-text reviews is made.

## Economic foundation

Friedman (1957), *A Theory of the Consumption Function*, chapter 3, provides the permanent-income distinction. It does not imply a universal declining finite-windfall spending share without additional assumptions about preferences, constraints, horizons and shock persistence. Primary source: https://www.nber.org/books-and-chapters/theory-consumption-function/permanent-income-hypothesis . The book chapter is additional to the 53-paper count.

## Five clusters

A — Size: cash-size declines are common but not universal; heterogeneous-agent models distinguish finite-change shares from local derivatives. Realized lottery/programme evidence and stated scenarios are informative about different objects.

B — Mental accounts: budgets, labels and small windfalls motivate a possible interpretation. None licenses inferring a psychological mediator from a declining Cash curve alone.

C — Form: use restrictions, expiry and liquidity can matter separately. Retail-category responses cannot be substituted for total additional consumption.

D — JEBO: related papers identify a concrete behavioral contrast and place its design before mechanisms. The outlet includes hypothetical outcomes, but this does not remove the need for a distinct contribution.

E — Measurement: reports about actual rebates, hypothetical allocations and transaction estimates are distinct. Direct bins differ from filter-first questions, especially near zero.

## Novelty gate — substantial overlap, narrower contribution

The broad priority claim fails. Bernard (2023) already crosses size and payment mode. His main specification is additive, but the six reported means themselves display unequal declines. Our defensible extension concerns category-restricted resources, three nominal amounts and explicit uncertainty around differential gradients. It is not the first joint design or the first decomposition. See equation (1) and Tables 2–4 in the [Bundesbank paper](https://www.bundesbank.de/resource/blob/909438/d24b53faa075049c3ba7fe0dcdf512b3/mL/2023-05-22-dkp-13-data.pdf).

No clean prior experiment with this exact Cash/Food/Medical design was verified in the bounded search. That absence is not proof of priority. Statistical evidence here does not establish a general interaction; choose Story C, with the three-form comparison as a qualified extension. This satisfies the repositioning gate rather than forcing Story A.

## Corrections to earlier framing

Remove claims of first joint randomization, novel conditional-margin discovery, universal MPC decline, or theoretical rejection of bindingness. Published Andreolli–Surico results include opposite size responses across resource groups. The Jappelli2026 published abstract and its2024 WP have different numeric estimates; cite only the common qualitative result. Crossley2026 and Bonomo2026 remain working papers. Parker–Souleles2019 validates reports about actual rebates, not all hypothetical instruments.
