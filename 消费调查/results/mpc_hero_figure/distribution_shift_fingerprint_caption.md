# Companion figure | Where is the Cash distribution different at the larger transfer?

For Cash (A), Food (B) and Medical (C), points show the approved six-bin probability at ¥5,000 minus that at ¥200, in percentage points. All panels share the same vertical scale and zero reference. Bin order is “basically no additional spending” (abbreviated “None”), <10%, 10–25%, 25–50%, 50–75% and >75%; shades match the hero atlas's response-intensity palette. Cash's >75% bin is 8.1 percentage points smaller, compared with 2.5 for Food and 1.9 for Medical. Cash's no-additional-spending bin changes by only −0.08 percentage points; its <10%, 10–25% and 50–75% bins have larger shares. Thus the largest visible Cash endpoint change occurs in the high tail, rather than the extensive margin.

Only aggregate subtraction of frozen shares is performed; no model, sample or inferential procedure changes. The frozen six-bin sources do not provide confidence intervals for these differences, so this descriptive fingerprint intentionally has none. Cell sizes at ¥200/¥5,000 are Cash 619/584, Food 614/602 and Medical 615/636 (endpoint sample N=3,670). Each participant answered one randomized scenario. Positive and negative differences describe independent cell distributions, not people moving between bins. The plot does not establish causal mediation or exclusive tail-specificity; the latter is not established by the frozen multiplicity-adjusted cross-threshold tests.
