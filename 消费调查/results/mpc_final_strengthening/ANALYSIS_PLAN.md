# Bounded final-pass implementation plan

Frozen before this pass's result generation (2026-10-02), based on MPC_SIZE_CURVE_STRENGTHENING_WORK.md and PR #9 d749f5c. No new features, moderators, sample definitions or mechanisms.

- Reuse PR #9 `prepare`, controls, mappings and samples directly; do not run its discovery pipeline or alter its files.
- Six-estimand Holm family per outcome/sample/adjustment: Cash, Food, Medical, equal-weight Restricted, Restricted−Cash, Food−Medical. Supplemental Food−Cash and Medical−Cash contrasts labelled outside this family. Primary reference remains R/unadjusted/ordinal; threshold findings cannot silently replace it.
- Binary GLM logit/probit: explicit HC1 score/Hessian sandwich. Balanced-form AME averages the derivative across all three assigned amount ranks with equal dose weights; adjusted models standardize over the same full analysis-sample covariate distribution for every form. Equal-weight Restricted AME averages Food/Medical AMEs, not their nonlinear coefficients. Delta method uses analytic gradients. Unrestricted models report endpoint probability changes, not continuous derivatives.
- All nonlinear marginal contrasts are model-based; adjusted CI conditions on empirical covariates. No new respondent-level predictions exported.
- 5,000 fixed-seed cell-stratified respondent bootstrap draws. Sampling multinomial counts from the six observed categories within each of nine cells is exactly the empirical respondent bootstrap for these category-based statistics; each cell retains its observed N. Same draws for threshold-vector covariance, relative endpoint scales and implied-yuan translation. No draws exported.
- Tail specificity: joint bootstrap covariance, Wald normal CI/p, Holm four direct top-minus-other-threshold contrasts within each comparison. Restricted−Cash primary; Food−Cash/Medical−Cash secondary. Optional global profile Wald uses four independent differences.
- Relative scales: pooled risk/odds calculated from equal-weight **probability mixtures**; ratio of ratios is not the average of Food/Medical ratios. Percentile bootstrap intervals on ratio scale; Wald p on log-ratio scale. Nine comparative tests (three contrasts × probability/risk/odds scale) Holm; descriptive within-form changes reported separately.
- Finite grid exactly 5 samples × 2 adjustments × 2 amount representations × 7 outcome representations × 3 contrasts = 420 specifications. No additional tuning/selection. Endpoint effects divided by two only in separate comparable-per-fivefold columns; raw endpoint effects retained. Ordinal/cardinal-like outcomes standardized by their fixed raw-sample SD for descriptive comparison, probability-scale effects kept separate, ordered latent scales never pooled. Outcome/sample/adjustment/model families all explicitly labelled.
- Ordered-model design removes explicit intercept and keeps the same fixed control block. HC1 score sandwich; convergence required. Ordered unrestricted contrasts are latent-index endpoint changes, not derivatives.
- Multinomial model is an unadjusted form×z category-probability diagnostic only; raw cells remain reference.
- Constant-dollar benchmark minimizes unweighted squared error across three raw midpoint means for K/T, reports fit error only. No structural inference.
- Exactly ten report sections and one of the three prescribed story judgments. No manuscript edits. Stop after this package; no further current-data exploratory analysis.
