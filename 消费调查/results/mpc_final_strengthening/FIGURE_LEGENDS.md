# Final-strengthening figure legends

All outcomes hypothetical **stated** responses. Different people occupy different randomized cells. Restricted is an equal-weight derived Food/Medical mean, not a fourth treatment. Points/lines describe three discrete doses; no intermediate dose response is identified. Intervals pointwise, not multiplicity-adjusted.

- **A — Pooled curves** (`figA_pooled_curves.png`). Midpoint share (left) and top-category probability (right); raw cell means, 5,000 stratified-bootstrap percentile CIs. Cash/Restricted emphasized, Food/Medical lightly shown. Source `figA_source.csv`: outcome/form/amount/estimate/lo/hi. Slope inference in `restricted_vs_cash_slopes.csv`.
- **B — Joint threshold profile** (`figB_threshold_profile.png`). Equal-weight Restricted−Cash trend difference at five nested thresholds, bootstrap covariance-based SE and 95% normal CI. Visual rise does not establish tail-specificity; direct contrasts in `threshold_tail_specificity.csv` are not significant after Holm. Source `figB_source.csv`: threshold/estimate/se/lo/hi. Joint covariance available separately.
- **C — Relative scales** (`figC_relative_scales.png`). Top row: within-form 200→5,000 top-category probability changes, risk ratios and odds ratios, including the derived Restricted mixture. Bottom row: between-form differences in changes, ratios of risk ratios and ratios of odds ratios. Null 0 in left panels, 1 in ratio panels. Stratified-bootstrap percentile intervals; p-values use bootstrap SD (log scale for ratios); nine-test Holm results in table, not encoded as corrected intervals. Source `figC_source.csv` contains all 21 summaries. Restricted ratio uses probability mixture before computing ratio.
- **D — Finite specification curve** (`figD_specification_curve.png`). Restricted−Cash only; three comparable coding panels shown separately: midpoint, top LPM, top logit probability marginal effects. Fixed order, no sorting by effect or significance. R/A/C/Q1/Q2; U=unadjusted, A=adjusted, T=trend, E=unrestricted endpoint divided by two for display. CI raw pointwise HC3/HC1 delta. Positive-spec proportions are descriptive. Source `figD_source.csv`; all 420 specifications, including ordinal and ordered latent models and other contrasts, in `specification_curve.csv`. Midpoint and probability panels not pooled statistically.
- **E — Share versus yuan** (`figE_share_vs_yuan.png`). Same midpoint-coded observations on share scale (left) and multiplied by assigned amount (right), with stratified-bootstrap percentile CIs. Share can fall while absolute implied spending rises strongly. This is not realized spending or independent confirmation. Source `figE_source.csv`: midpoint/CI and implied_yuan/CI at each form/amount.
