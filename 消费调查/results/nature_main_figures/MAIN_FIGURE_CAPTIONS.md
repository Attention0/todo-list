## Figure 1 | Transfer-form differences in the stated MPC-size profile

Cash displays the steepest descriptive decline across three representations of the elicited spending response. **A**, mean original ordinal response category (individual responses range1-6; higher categories indicate a larger stated share spent). **B**, approximate stated MPC using fixed category midpoints0,0.05,0.175,0.375,0.625,0.875. **C**, probability of the highest category (>75% stated spending). Points are unadjusted randomized-cell means in the full R sample (N=5,497; cell N=584-636). Participants were independently assigned one of three forms and one of three amounts, not repeatedly measured. Error bars are approved pointwise95% intervals: A, mean +/-1.96 sample-mean SE (PR9); B/C, existing5,000 nine-cell stratified-bootstrap percentile intervals (PR10). Lines connect three discrete randomized amounts, with equal spacing reflecting fivefold amount steps; they do not identify a continuous dose-response or individual change. Profiles are descriptive: flatter Food/Medical means do not establish zero slopes or equivalence; the original ordinal estimand is not replaced by a tail outcome.
