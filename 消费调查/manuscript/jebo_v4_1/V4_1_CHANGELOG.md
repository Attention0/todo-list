Existing literature is organized by its contribution to this question; all 25 references remain unchanged.
- Sections 4–5: retain the numerical tables and four-figure sequence. Cash top contraction, diffuse Food changes and Medical offsets remain central. Clarify independent-cell comparisons and convergence without distributional identity.
- Section 6: reduced from 1,746 to 1,085 words. Each subsection now starts with an economic idea, gives the relevant evidence and ends with an interim conclusion. The sharp Food prediction is distinguished from broader bindingness accounts.
- Section 7: develops resource categorization as the preferred organizing interpretation, connecting Cash, Food and Medical explicitly. The preference is interpretive, not a tested ranking; categorization and perceived spendability were not measured.
- Discussion: contribution first, limitations consolidated, substantive ending. Added a short Conclusion rather than ending with a methodological retreat.
- Highlights: five concise statements, each within 85 characters.

## Logic corrections rather than stylistic preferences

Removed individual-level movement language from independent-cell comparisons; removed attribution of mean convergence to one common middle-bin frequency; distinguished ordinal-score inference from whole-distribution dominance; qualified questionnaire numbering versus confirmed field order. Kept mean stability distinct from distributional stability, and the scenario's persistent Medical balance distinct from unmeasured mental categorization. These corrections change exposition, not estimates or conclusions. Detailed review appears in `V4_1_FINAL_AUDIT.md`.

## Scientific boundaries preserved

Corrected within-Cash midpoint/highest-category evidence remains stronger than ordinal or cross-form interaction evidence. Descriptive convergence is not a proven population interaction. Nonrejection is not equivalence. Midpoint yuan and affine slopes are not realized spending or structural MPCs. Bottom-category coding is not observed participation. Food proxy results do not exclude all bindingness models. Imprecise moderators do not establish homogeneity. The instrument is hypothetical, unincentivized and bundled; author/fieldwork/ethics information remains pending.
