# JEBO v4 story memo

Writing authority: main `dc020b1`, JEBO_V4_WRITING_SPEC/WORK. Scientific base:
PR19 `363313c8adc52cce252c11390a32e46c7a98d25f`. This memo precedes drafting.

**Question and three puzzles.** Does transfer form matter equally across small
and large amounts? Cash provides a familiar declining finite-windfall share
benchmark. The puzzles are Medical's nearly flat mean (17.73 to17.84%), Food's
weaker decline than Cash (22.70 to19.49 versus25.30 to20.16%), and the compression
of mean form gaps between RMB200 and5,000. These are descriptive randomized-cell
patterns; a universal population interaction is a stronger, unresolved claim.

**Scale.** This is a percentage-allocation puzzle. Cash implied yuan grows from
50.60 to1,008.19; its finite incremental responses are.220 and.195. Food's.193/.194
and Medical's.179/.178 clarify why declining shares need not imply falling yuan
or sharply different marginal responses. Affine descriptions aid interpretation
but neither establish equivalent slopes nor identify structural MPCs.

**Anatomy before explanation.** Cash loses8.09pp in the highest response bin,
with an almost unchanged bottom share. Food reallocates more diffusely from
middle-high and high shares to lower/middle shares. Medical's flat mean combines
lower bottom and top shares with larger intermediate shares. These independent
cell comparisons do not track respondents moving between bins. At5,000 the
10–25% frequencies are24.48/23.87/23.50%, yet bottom and upper bins differ.
Mean convergence is not distributional identity, and no new distribution test
will be added. Figure3 will show only endpoint category-share subtraction.

**Progressive explanation.** Standard size dependence establishes the Cash
benchmark; yuan/affine arithmetic defines the object; bottom-bin behavior
constrains one simple descriptive account. Food bindingness then supplies a
sharp sign prediction, contradicted conditional on its coarse proxy. Category
need, relative income/liquidity and observable-type diagnostics supply further
questions, but their nulls remain precision-limited. None selects a psychological
account by eliminating all competitors.

**Unified candidate.** Small Cash can be treated as readily spendable, while
Food/Medical arrive partly categorized. Larger amounts may invite broader
allocation across spending, saving, debt and future needs, compressing the small
Cash high-response premium. Different starting categories could allow Medical's
offsetting distributional changes. This accommodates the pattern; it does not
uniquely predict Medical's cancellation or identify perceived spendability.
Future work must separately manipulate label, restriction, expiry and liquidity,
fix the horizon and observe allocation intentions and realized transactions.

**Hard boundaries.** Cash midpoint/Top75 survive family42; ordinal does not.
Cross-form interaction remains uncertain. Bottom decomposition is a coding
identity. Interval estimates depend on scale assumptions. Form bundles and
hypothetical outcomes remain explicit. Existing statistics, samples, correction
families and source files are immutable. Missing author metadata stay pending.
