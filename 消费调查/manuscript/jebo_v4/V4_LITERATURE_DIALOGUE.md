The conceptual spine is Friedman (1957), Jappelli and Pistaferri (2010), and Kaplan and Violante (2022): cash decline is compatible with smoothing, but neither monotonicity nor our form interaction follows generally. Fagereng et al. (2021) supplies direct balance-sheet and lottery evidence. Thaler (1985, 1999), Shefrin and Thaler (1988), and Heath and Soll (1996) motivate account assignment and category budgets, without turning the current outcome into a spendability measure.

The form cluster distinguishes labeling (Kooreman; Abeler–Marklein), eligible-category expenditure and inframarginality (Hastings–Shapiro; Cunha), and a real shopping-voucher programme (Kan–Peng–Wang). This distinction is essential: substitution for planned eligible purchases can free cash without creating the additional total consumption asked about here.

The two JEBO papers are writing references because each begins with an economic behavior and a concrete manipulation or comparison, then explains its institutional meaning. V4 follows that question-first approach. It does not borrow their causal strength, mechanism identification or outcome validation.
